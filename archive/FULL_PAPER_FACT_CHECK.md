### NEW finding 1 — Section H missingness: the 53-row "gap" is overlap double-counting, not a missing category

`results/round5_part_H_missingness.json` reported `check_sum.total = 44,136` against a true
`dropna`-union of 44,083 — a 53-row overcount — and left `other_beyond_captured: 380`
un-itemized. We extended the original logic
(`src/round5_factcheck_H_missing.py`, renamed in this reorganization to `src/verification/verify_followup_resolution.py`) to do a proper
set decomposition rather than a naive additive sum.

**Decomposition (canonical `modeling_dataset_canonical_v2.parquet`, N = 514,618):**

| Component | Size | Overlap with the others |
|-----------|------|-------------------------|
| farf join-failure group (yearbuilt & commfar & residfar & facilfar all null) | 31,692 | 0 with numfloors-beyond (definitionally disjoint) |
| numfloors-beyond-farf (numfloors null AND not farf) | 11,930 | **53 rows also have dem missing** |
| dem-unique-to-dem_share (dem null AND not farf) | 134 | includes the **53 numfloors-beyond rows** |
| other-beyond-captured (target_log_price/landuse/BOROUGH null, not already captured) | 380 | 0 with all three of the above |
| **Naive additive sum of the 4** | **44,136** | — |
| **Proper union (\|A∪B∪C∪D\|)** | **44,083** | = exact true `dropna`-union (Y) |

The script's `dem_unique_to_dem_share` was defined as "dem missing AND NOT farf" — but that
definition does **not** exclude the numfloors-beyond rows. So the 53 rows that are missing
**both** numfloors **and** dem_share (but are **not** in the farf group) get counted once inside
`numfloors_beyond_farf = 11,930` and a second time inside `dem_unique_to_dem_share = 134`.
The `other_beyond_captured: 380` rows, by contrast, are genuinely disjoint from all three other
categories (overlap with each = 0); they are real "other" drops (target_log_price / landuse /
BOROUGH null in rows not already captured). Decomposing the 380 by variable: 0 of them are
*only* missing target_log_price, and 0 overlap with numfloors-beyond, dem, or farf — so the 380
are spread across target_log_price+other and landuse/BOROUGH combinations, none reintroducing a
prior component.

**Conclusion:** the gap 44,136 − 44,083 = 53 is entirely the overlap between `numfloors_beyond_farf`
and `dem_unique_to_dem_share`. Once numfloors is corrected to 11,930 (per Round 2), the four named
components **no longer sum additively to the total** — they overlap. The *union* of the four
still equals the true 44,083 exactly.

**Additional manuscript patch sentence (standard old-text/new-text format):**
- old: `Together, a join-failure group of 31,692 rows, an additional 11,930 rows missing numfloors, 134 rows uniquely missing dem_share, and 380 other rows account for the 44,083 rows dropped before regression.`
- new: `The 44,083 rows dropped before regression arise from overlapping missingness patterns: a join-failure group of 31,692 rows, an additional 11,930 rows missing numfloors (beyond that group), 134 rows uniquely missing dem_share, and 380 other rows; the four named components overlap (53 rows are missing both numfloors and dem_share and are counted in two of the four) and so do not decompose into cleanly additive components, but their union equals 44,083 exactly.`

(Where the exact "old" wording must match what's in the paper; above phrased as the canonical
representative — adjust to the exact manuscript sentence.)

## 5. What this pass cannot do on its own (caveats)

   numbers is correctly phrased, whether caveats are appropriately hedged, whether figures are
   legible, or whether the narrative argument flows. A substantive human read-through remains
   required before publication, even after this pass reports all-green.
